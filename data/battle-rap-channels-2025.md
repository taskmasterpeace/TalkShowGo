## Intelligence Strategies

### 1. Long-Form Interviews (10-15+ minutes)
- Prioritize interview content over short reaction clips
- Battlers explaining their own perspective = primary source intelligence
- Search for: `"[battler name] interview battle rap"` filtered by duration

### 2. Comment Consensus
- High-engagement comments indicate community sentiment
- Comment likes/replies show which takes resonate
- Cross-reference comments across multiple channels for consensus

### 3. Cross-Referencing for Verification
- Multiple channels discussing the same topic = confirmed story
- Verify battles, allegations, news with web searches
- Don't publish unverified claims (see: Chef Trez vs Tsu Surf hallucination)

### 4. Entity Tracking
- Monitor battler birthdays for "happy birthday" coverage
- Track announcements, milestones, career moves
- Build entity metadata: real names, hometowns, affiliations

### 5. Focus on Comments, Not Just Transcripts
- URLTV battle transcripts ≠ news (just the battles themselves)
- Blogger video comments = community reactions and sentiment
- Comment section is where the culture's voice lives
