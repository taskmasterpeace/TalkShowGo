THE HUDDLE — SEAT 2 AUDITION (replacing Big Mike Vega)
Three candidates. All built to the same spec: substance FIRST, energy on top —
every take lands a "because," never a catchphrase in place of an argument.
The reel plays them in order: A, then B, then C.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CANDIDATE A — DRE CALLAWAY ("Big Dre")
Who: Ex-NFL offensive guard, nine seasons in the trenches, two of them in Atlanta.
     Film-room guy. Been in the actual building, knows what locker rooms sound like.
Voice: Deep, warm, gravelly. Big chest laugh. Southern-tinged. Mid-40s.
How he argues: From the line of scrimmage out. Protections, fronts, what the tape
     actually shows. Calls out vibes-based takes by name.
Sample lines (Tua vs Penix):
  • "Protection tells you who starts. Tua's getting the ball out in under three
    seconds behind THIS line. You don't hand a rebuilt knee a five-step drop in
    Week 1 — that's not caution, that's math."
  • "I love the kid's arm too. But I played next to guys coming off that surgery,
    and the knee ain't the problem — the TRUST in the knee is. That takes live reps
    you can't fake in a walkthrough."

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CANDIDATE B — THEO MARSH
Who: Ex-college quarterback turned scheme-and-numbers analyst. Early 30s.
     Quick, precise, a little cocky — but he shows his work every single time.
Voice: Crisp, bright, fast but controlled. Playful edge.
How he argues: "The film and the numbers say the same thing — and I brought both."
     Rate stats, snap counts, route trees. Kills a bad take with one specific.
Sample lines (Tua vs Penix):
  • "Penix has zero eleven-on-eleven reps this camp. Zero. Tua's out-snapped him all
    of August. You don't win Week 1 with upside, you win it with reps — and the reps
    say Tua, full stop."
  • "Everybody's quoting the GM's 'insurance policy' line like it's poetry. It's not
    poetry, it's a depth chart — and insurance sits on the bench until something breaks."

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CANDIDATE C — RAY DILLARD ("Uncle Ray")
Who: Atlanta lifer. Watching this franchise since the Dirty Birds, survived the
     Vick era, 28-3, all of it. The barbershop historian. Late 50s.
Voice: Rich Southern drawl, unhurried storyteller cadence, wry. A little weathered.
How he argues: History IS his evidence. "We've seen this movie" — then he names the
     year, the quarterback, and how it ended. Funny, but the receipts are real.
Sample lines (Tua vs Penix):
  • "We tried romance at quarterback in this town before, and I've got the scars to
    prove it. Start the grown man, let the young fella heal. Atlanta knows exactly
    what rushing the future costs — we paid that bill in 2007."
  • "Eight straight years out of the playoffs, and y'all want to gamble the opener
    on a knee brace? Baby, I've seen this team lose games it scheduled to win."
