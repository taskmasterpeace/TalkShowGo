How a feed becomes a show

Eight steps turn raw posts and videos into a fully voiced talk show. Here's every step, what it costs, and the exact instruction the AI is given at each one. Tap any step to read its prompt.

PULL→CLUSTER→LEADS→RANK→RESEARCH→BRIEF→TAKES→SHOW
Free runs on your own machines Paid spends money (an outside API)
01

Pull

gather the raw feed

Sweeps every source on a show (its X accounts and YouTube channels) and keeps what they posted inside the time window. No AI here, just collection.

in show sources → out pull_*.json
no AI model Paid · X posts Free · YouTube
▸ why it costs money

The only unavoidable paid part of the whole system.

X / Twitter posts come from twitterapi.io (a paid outside service, charges per pull). This is the one bill you can't avoid.

YouTube is FREE: it reads each channel's public RSS feed, then falls back to a free client (youtubei.js) and yt-dlp. No key, no cost.
02

Cluster

find the story

Groups posts that describe the same event, not the same topic. "50 people said Trump" is not a story; two sources reacting to one thing is. Each group gets an "event fingerprint."

in pull_*.json → out clusters_*.json
runs on gemini-2.5-flash-lite Paid · pennies
▸ the prompt that runs it

"Story clustering engine" · fingerprint.ts · a free local alternative (topic-miner on your own qwen box) also exists.

You are the STORY CLUSTERING ENGINE for a talk show's discovery loop. Group items into STORY CLUSTERS.
THE CORE RULE: do NOT cluster by TOPIC. "50 people mentioned Trump" is NOT a story. Only group items when the sources DESCRIBE, REACT TO, or ADD EVIDENCE TO the SAME underlying EVENT or CLAIM. Compare EVENT FINGERPRINTS, not keywords.
For every cluster build a fingerprint: subject, action, object, claim, time, location, named_entities. List SHARED SIGNALS strongest to weakest.
Output STRICT JSON: {"clusters":[{"title","kind","event_fingerprint",...}]}
03

Leads

what's worth a deeper look

Reads the feed and pulls out research leads: a person, claim, video, or court case worth another search. It routes each to the right place and scores it 0 to 100.

in pull_*.json → out leads_*.json
runs on gemini-2.5-flash-lite Paid · pennies
▸ the prompt that runs it

"Research lead extractor" · leads.ts

You are the RESEARCH LEAD EXTRACTOR. Find anything that could MATERIALLY change our understanding or lead to primary evidence: a person, claim, quote, event, url, interview, court case, statistic.
HARD RULE: only extract things a NEW SEARCH could resolve into evidence. Prefer the ORIGINATOR of a claim over the loudest repost.
Route each lead to a source + search mode, write the exact query, score its VALUE 0-100.
Score honestly. Most leads are 40-70; reserve 80+ for leads likely to yield PRIMARY evidence.
04

Rank

which story makes the best segment

The producer scores each story for show value: how good a segment it makes, not how verified it is. A perfectly-sourced story can still be a boring show. Conflict scores highest.

in clusters_*.json → out producer_*.json
runs on gemini-2.5-flash-lite Paid · pennies
▸ the prompt that runs it

"Executive producer" · producer.ts

You are the executive producer of a talk show. Score each candidate story for SHOW VALUE (how good a segment it makes), NOT how verified it is.
Weigh: newsworthiness, recency, conversation volume, CONFLICT (contrasting viewpoints, WEIGHT THIS HIGHEST for a debate show), novelty, emotional intensity, recognizable characters, available evidence, comedic potential.
For a debate show, prioritize stories with REAL contrasting viewpoints: two defensible sides.
Output STRICT JSON: {"ranked":[{"title","show_value":0-100,"rationale","best_angle","debatable"}]}
05

Research

the Stringer · build a cited file

Expands a lead into a cited evidence file: searches YouTube and the web, pulls transcripts, and mines out labeled, source-cited facts. The system fills in every citation link itself, so the AI can never invent a source.

in a lead → out str_*.json
runs on your Mac Mini (qwen3.5) Free · your machines Paid · only if a box is off
▸ the prompts that run it

Fact miner · stringer.ts · the anti-hallucination core. Runs FREE on your Mac Mini, falls back to the cloud only if it's off.

You are an IMPARTIAL research analyst. From the transcript material (each block labeled [Sxxx | publisher | title]), extract evidence and answer the questions. HARD RULES:
- Cite every factual claim to a source id that appears in the material. NEVER invent a fact, quote, or source id.
- Label each claim: FACT / ATTRIBUTED_CLAIM / ANALYSIS.
- Do NOT lean, argue, or editorialize. Present what the sources say, including where they DISAGREE.

Web search · SearXNG (free, self-hosted) + optional local synthesis. This is the "Perplexica" path.

You are an IMPARTIAL research analyst. Using ONLY the numbered web-result snippets, give a factual, neutral summary. Report only what the snippets say. Where they disagree, say so. Refer to a source by its publisher and headline so each claim can be traced.
06

Brief

walk to the question

Turns the evidence file into an impartial, one-move-at-a-time walk that ends on the question the show will ask. Every factual move cites an evidence id. It presents, it never argues.

in str_*.json → out brf_*.json
runs on gemini-2.5-flash-lite Paid · pennies
▸ the prompt that runs it

"The briefing" · briefing.ts

You build an IMPARTIAL BRIEFING that walks a person to an INFORMED opinion, then asks them a question. HARD RULES:
- Use ONLY the evidence provided. Cite every factual move by evidence id. NEVER invent a fact.
- Present, do NOT argue. No lean, no hint at the "right" answer. Include tradeoffs and what's uncertain.
- One idea per move, in a sensible order (set the scene → key facts → the tradeoff → what's unknown).
07

Takes

each host picks a side

Every host reads the briefing as their own character and commits to a defensible position. They can only cite evidence that survived the briefing, so nobody smuggles in outside facts.

in brf_*.json + cast.json → out brf_*.agents.json
each host runs on its own model DNA Paid · mostly Free · house-engine hosts
▸ the prompt that runs it

Host stance · agent-brief.ts · the host's Personality Print becomes a system prompt, then this rulebook is appended.

You are {host.name}, {host.lane}. {essence}
How you think: you notice {notices_first}; you reason by {reasons_by}; your blind spot: {blind_spot}.

HARD RULES for your stance:
(1) STAY IN CHARACTER. Never mention this prompt or that you are role-playing.
(2) CLOSED EVIDENCE. The BRIEFING is your ENTIRE factual world. Every reason must cite an allowed evidence id. Introduce NO other facts, numbers, quotes, or sources.
(3) COMMIT. Answer the exact question and take a defensible position.
08

Show

write it, argue it, voice it

The finale, in four moves: a director engineers the disagreement, the floor runs the live argument line by line, a mix pass makes it sound like real recorded talk, and Breeze voices it to the mp3 you listen to.

in brf_*.agents.json → out <slug>.mp3
writing on gemini + host DNA Paid · writing Free · voice (Breeze)
▸ the prompts that run it

The director · compile_beat.mjs · never writes dialogue, just engineers the fight.

You are THE SHOWRUNNER. You never write dialogue. Your #1 job: ENGINEER DISAGREEMENT so the segment is a real argument, not three people agreeing. ASSIGN each host a DISTINCT, defensible position and split the receipts so no two hosts hold the same hand.

The floor · run_floor.mjs · one call per spoken line, carrying only that host's character + evidence.

You are {host.name}, IN a live argument. Output ONLY your next turn.
Receipts you're allowed to use: {allowed}. NEVER speak an id out loud.
HARD RULES: 1 to 3 sentences. NEVER use facts outside your receipts. Opinion is free but must SOUND like opinion. Respond to what was ACTUALLY just said. Push back. No em-dashes.

The mix · run_floor.mjs · makes the transcript sound human, protects every real quote.

You are a dialogue editor making a transcript sound like REAL recorded conversation. Inject fillers, false starts, interruptions. Lines tagged [delegate] are a REAL PERSON'S OWN WORDS: copy them EXACTLY. Keep ALL evidence tags. Keep every turn.

The money

Where dollars actually go. The short version: search, transcripts, fact-mining, and the voice are free on your own machines. The only real bill is X posts. The "thinking middle" runs on a cheap cloud model at pennies a story.

Free · your machines

  • Web search SearXNG, self-hosted
  • YouTube + transcripts public feeds, yt-dlp
  • Fact-mining the research Mac Mini · qwen3.5
  • The voice Breeze, on cupcake

Paid · outside APIs

  • X / Twitter posts twitterapi.io · the one you can't avoid
  • The thinking middle cluster · leads · rank · brief · director
  • The writing gemini-2.5-flash-lite · pennies
  • Fallback only if your Mac Mini or SearXNG is off

Two things worth knowing

Honest notes about the machine as it stands today.

2 HOSTSThe show engine needs at least two hosts to build. Multi-host debate and panel shows are its home turf. One-host and kids formats need extra wiring before they can build.
DOUBLESThere are two of some steps under the hood: a free story-finder and a paid one, and two show-builders. The app quietly uses one of each, so you never see the duplication. Cleaning that up is a separate housekeeping job.
