# WHAT HAPPENED AT KAI CENAT'S HOUSE — RESEARCH BRIEF (compiled 2026-08-31)

**THE EVENT:** On or about **Aug 25-26, 2026**, a woman calling herself **Yasmin** pulled up to the gate of Kai Cenat's house (the AMP house) at ~10 a.m. with a **~2-year-old girl** and told him the child is his daughter. She said she **drove 15 hours** to confront him. Kai — woken up by two calls from his own security — came out, denied ever meeting her, **filmed the confrontation on his phone**, and later that day told the whole story on stream while airing the footage. The child, asked "who's your dad?", pointed at Kai and said **"You"** — and Kai's response, **"Oh, she trained the baby. Like, this baby a paid actor,"** became the meme of the week. He offered to take a **DNA test live on stream**, then ended the standoff by giving the woman and child **snacks and water and putting them in an Uber** back to their hotel. Evidence ledger: `kai_cenat_evidence.json` (E1-E17).
