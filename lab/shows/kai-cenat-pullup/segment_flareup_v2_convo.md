## Why this sounds different from Script A (the checklist made audible)
- **Uneven turn lengths** — Tasha wins the segment with 3-6 word turns; KK is silent except backchannels + one drop (turn-length distribution is a realism metric, not a style choice)
- **Interruptions land mid-clause**, twice at the exact moment the other host commits ("...why the man is HIM, T. That is why..." / "THANK you! Court adjourned...")
- **Withheld evidence detonates on the floor** — Blaze genuinely doesn't see [E14] coming; his "...that's real?" is the reveal landing live
- **Backchannels from the third chair** ("Mm." / "Whew.") instead of everyone taking full turns
- **False starts + resets** ("A rece... no. No no no." / "I... okay. Okay okay okay.")
- **A callback used as ammunition** (paid actor [E4]) instead of a catchphrase
- **Nobody validates anybody** — Tasha concedes only the FRAME, the argument stays unresolved, exit is a tease
- Evidence law intact: every factual claim still carries its tag; care rails unchanged
