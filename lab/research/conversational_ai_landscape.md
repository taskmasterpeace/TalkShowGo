# Conversational AI Landscape — Making Multi-Host AI Talk Sound REAL
**Research date: 2026-08-31.** Scope: what currently (2025–2026) makes multi-host AI conversation sound like real people talking — for an AI talk show with locked host personalities. Organized in the 6 assigned areas; TOP 5 "steal this" list at the end.

**The one-sentence thesis that emerged:** every system that actually sounds real splits the problem into (a) a *script layer* engineered for disagreement, tension, and turn dynamics, and (b) an *audio layer* where a single-pass multi-speaker model performs the whole exchange and adds the disfluencies, overlaps, breaths, and laughter itself. Nobody good writes "um" into a script and reads it with per-speaker TTS clips.

## 1. NotebookLM Audio Overviews (Google) — the benchmark

### How it actually works (best available teardowns)
- Pipeline (5 stages): source ingestion → grounded analysis (Gemini) → narrative planning → dialogue scripting (two host roles get turns, questions, reframes) → **audio generation by a multi-speaker audio model** that performs the dialogue with natural timing. Teardown: https://roballandale.com/briefs/notebooklm-audio-overviews-workflow-teardown/
- **The killer detail (from the Latent Space interview with the NotebookLM team — Raiza Martin + Usama Bin Shafqat, late 2024):** the micro-interjections ("Oh really?", "Totally"), the "uh…", the pauses — **the disfluencies are built into the AUDIO MODEL, not generated by the LLM in the transcript.** The audio model handles intonation, breathing, pauses, and laughter. https://www.latent.space/p/notebooklm
- The audio model is believed to be in the **SoundStorm/AudioLM lineage** (Google DeepMind); DeepMind's audio-generation post describes producing 2 minutes of multi-speaker dialogue in <3s on a TPU v5e, with disfluencies and realistic turn dynamics. https://deepmind.google/blog/pushing-the-frontiers-of-audio-generation/ · https://grokipedia.com/page/soundstorm
- **Script-layer design principles from the team itself** (Latent Space):
  - "If there's just too much agreement between people, that's not fun" — engagement is engineered via **disagreement**.
  - The script **withholds information strategically** — details unroll gradually for narrative tension instead of front-loading conclusions.
  - Hosts deliberately have **no names and no backstories** (Steven Johnson's constraint), and the team keeps "a bit of imperfection" on purpose.
  - Eval was **taste-driven listening sessions**, with "entertaining" as the core (hard-to-quantify) metric.
- The messiness is the trick: an analysis of 200 NotebookLM podcasts notes the model "intentionally leaves in breaths, pauses, and overlapping speech — this messiness tricks the human brain into believing it is real," while the *content* rhythm becomes formulaic fast ("deep dive" tic, predictable validate-and-reframe pattern). https://medium.com/@bamby_media/i-listened-to-200-notebook-lm-podcasts-so-you-dont-have-to-d5b206911592

### Current capabilities (Aug 2026)
- **Formats:** Deep Dive, Brief, Critique, **Debate** (two hosts take opposing perspectives) — added Sept 2025. https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/03/googles-notebooklm-now-lets-you-customize-the-tone-of-its-ai-podcasts · https://aitoolinsight.com/notebooklm-brief-critique-debate-audio-formats/
- Steering prompt ("Customize"), length control, 50+ languages (Apr 2025), Interactive Mode (you can join the conversation live and ask the hosts questions). https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16212820 · https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2025/04/language-expansion-audio-overviews-notebooklm.html
- 2026 additions: **new voice options** for Audio Overviews, Cinematic Video Overviews (Mar 2026, Ultra tier), notebook copying incl. Studio artifacts (Aug 17, 2026). https://notebooklm-guide.com/notebooklm-updates/ · https://felloai.com/notebooklm-update-1m-token-chat-goals-saved-history/
- **Limits that matter for us:** still one fixed two-host format — **no custom personas, no more-than-2 hosts, no script editing, no self-serve API** (consumer). Google has preview Enterprise APIs and *deprecated* its Podcast API; the unofficial ecosystem (AutoContent API) wraps NotebookLM-style generation as a REST service. https://www.jellypod.com/blog/best-notebooklm-alternatives · https://autocontentapi.com/blog/does-notebooklm-have-an-api

**Takeaway:** NotebookLM proves the two-layer architecture, but is a closed box with locked hosts — it cannot be our stack; it is our quality bar and design doc.

## 2. Open-source NotebookLM-style podcast generators

- **Podcastfy** (github.com/souzatharsis/podcastfy) — the OG open-source Python alternative (Apache-ish, pip-installable). Text/URL/PDF/YouTube/image → conversational transcript → TTS. 100+ LLMs supported for the transcript; conversation style, roles, structure, and length are **config-customizable** (`conversation_config`), shortform or 30+ min longform. Better as a developer library than a product. https://github.com/souzatharsis/podcastfy · https://pypi.org/project/podcastfy
- **Open Notebook** (github.com/lfnovo/open-notebook) — the currently-hot (2026) self-hosted NotebookLM: ~28K GitHub stars as of mid-2026 (+3.9K in one week), 18+ AI providers (incl. Ollama local, ElevenLabs), full podcast-episode generation from a source bundle. This is the most *alive* self-hosted option. https://github.com/lfnovo/open-notebook/releases · https://dev.to/andrew-ooo/open-notebook-review-self-hosted-notebooklm-alternative-1210
- **PodAgent** (research, ACL Findings 2025) — the best documented **dialogue-generation approach** to steal: a **Host–Guest–Writer multi-agent writers' room.** Host-agent invents guests + interview outline; Guest-agents answer *in role* from their perspective; Writer-agent compiles the coherent script; then voice-role matching from a voice pool + LLM-guided expressive synthesis. Beat direct GPT-4 generation significantly on topic-discussion quality; 87.4% voice-matching accuracy. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00455 · https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1226/
- **PodEval** (Oct 2025) — open evaluation framework for podcast audio generation (text/speech/audio dimensions, real-podcast comparison corpus). Useful if we want a repeatable "does it sound real" eval instead of vibes. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00485
- **AutoContent API** — commercial REST API producing NotebookLM-style podcasts (credit-based); the pragmatic "just give me the NotebookLM sound via API" option. https://autocontentapi.com/
- Hosted product alternative: **Jellypod** (see §5) is the leader for *custom multi-host personas*.

**Takeaway:** the open projects all still generate a clean turn-taking script and then per-line TTS — exactly the "written dialogue" problem we have. The stealable parts are PodAgent's writers'-room decomposition and Podcastfy's conversation_config, not their audio layers.

## 3. Multi-agent conversation / persona frameworks

### The big frameworks, 2026 status
- **AutoGen → maintenance mode (Oct 2025).** Split three ways: legacy AutoGen; **Microsoft Agent Framework** (GA April 2026 — Microsoft's production lane); and the community fork **AG2**. AG2's signature **GroupChat pattern — multiple agents share one conversation and a *selector* decides who speaks next — is the closest primitive to a talk-show floor manager.** AG2 2026 Beta added streaming + event-driven architecture. Best for experimental conversational dynamics, not production hosting. https://atlan.com/know/ai-agent/what-is-autogen/ · https://qubittool.com/blog/ai-agent-framework-comparison-2026
- **LangGraph** — the production-readiness leader in 2026 (checkpointing, streaming, LangSmith observability). Right choice if the show pipeline needs a durable orchestration graph; it contributes nothing specific to *conversational realism*. https://gurusup.com/blog/best-multi-agent-frameworks-2026
- **DeepMind Concordia v2.0** — generative social simulation, rebuilt on a **tabletop-RPG-style Entity-Component architecture**: a Game-Master narrates/adjudicates and agents with personalities, memories, and goals interact in natural language; scenarios assembled from reusable components without new code. The GM pattern is a strong model for a "showrunner" agent that sets scenes and provokes conflict. https://github.com/google-deepmind/concordia · https://www.cooperativeai.com/post/google-deepmind-releases-concordia-library-v2-0
- **Microsoft TinyTroupe 0.6.0 (Feb 1, 2026)** — LLM persona simulation with the deepest *persona specification* machinery: rich TinyPerson definitions (personality traits, beliefs, preferences), **action-correction mechanisms for persona adherence/self-consistency**, and even statistical validation of simulations vs empirical data. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09788 · https://github.com/microsoft/TinyTroupe
- **Generative Agents lineage / AI Town (a16z)** — still alive (repo activity into July 2026); by 2026 these are research instruments for emergent social behavior. Known 2026 bottleneck: memory (context limits past ~50 agents; fixes are hierarchical memory + sharding). https://github.com/a16z-infra/ai-town
- **CAMEL** — the original role-playing multi-agent framework; still referenced but not a 2026 leader for this use case (not deeply re-verified this pass).

### The AI-streamer stacks (closest living relatives of an "AI talk show")
- **Neuro-sama** (Vedal's closed stack; top-subscribed Twitch streamer) — the documented lessons: **personality is embedded in model weights via iterative fine-tuning on real deployment interactions, not prompt engineering**; separate subsystems (LLM, vision, game agents, TTS, Live2D) coordinated in a continuous inference loop; long-term memory via RAG to hold a stable self-image across years. The Neuro + Evil Neuro twin format is effectively a working 2-host AI show with genuine interruptions because it's *live and reactive*, not scripted. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuro-sama · https://lin-guanguo.github.io/llm-memory-research/neuro-sama.research/
- **Project AIRI** (github.com/moeru-ai/airi) — open-source, MIT, explicitly "wishing to achieve Neuro-sama's altitude": realtime voice chat, Live2D, Minecraft/Factorio play; ~45K stars as of v0.11.3 (July 2026). The most serious open Neuro-clone. https://github.com/moeru-ai/airi
- **Open-LLM-VTuber** — local, cross-platform AI companion w/ voice interruption + Live2D; curated ecosystem list at https://github.com/proj-airi/awesome-ai-vtubers. https://github.com/Open-LLM-VTuber/Open-LLM-VTuber
- **Fable's Showrunner** ("Netflix of AI," Amazon Alexa Fund investment, public launch after 10K-user alpha, 2025) — prompt → full animated episode with dialogue, voices, editing; targets a playable movie in 2026. Proof that persona-locked episodic AI *shows* are a product category now. https://variety.com/2025/digital/news/netflix-of-ai-amazon-invests-fable-showrunner-launch-1236471989/

### 2026 research directly on our problem (script-layer realism)
- **"Learning to Interrupt in Language-based Multi-agent Communication"** (arXiv 2604.06452, Apr 2026) — trains agents *when to interrupt* in multi-agent chat rather than waiting for turns. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.06452
- **DUPLEXGEN** (arXiv 2607.26178, Jul 2026) — synthesizes turn-taking dialogues by **augmenting clean scripts with backchannels ("uh-huh," "right"), truncating utterances and overlapping the next turn, and inserting [PAUSE] hesitations** — a recipe we can copy verbatim as a post-pass on our scripts. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.26178
- Full-duplex survey ("From Turn-Taking to Synchronous Dialogue," 2025): the field-wide statement of why strict turn-taking sounds fake. https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14515v1

## 4. Character/persona platforms and specs

- **Character Card V3 (CCv3)** — the de-facto community persona standard, supported by SillyTavern (which writes V2+V3 chunks for compatibility). Spec: https://github.com/kwaroran/character-card-spec-v3/blob/main/SPEC_V3.md — V3 adds, over V2:
  - `assets` (icons, backgrounds, **emotion/expression variants**, audio/video via the **CHARX** zip format) — a locked host can ship with its face set and stingers in one file.
  - `group_only_greetings` (**required field; explicitly built for multi-character group scenes**), `nickname`, `creator_notes_multilingual`, `source`, timestamps.
  - `character_book` (lorebook) with **decorators** (`@@depth`, `@@activate_only_after`, `@@position`…) — conditional knowledge injection, e.g. running gags that only activate every N turns.
  - Formats: PNG/APNG tEXt chunk, bare JSON, or CHARX (zip of card.json + assets).
- **V2 vs V3 in practice:** V3 is "the new meta… built for complex, multi-character world-building"; V2 fine for standalone bots. https://abolitus.com/blog/sillytavern-character-cards-v2-vs-v3
- **SillyTavern group chats** are a working reference implementation of multi-character conversation management (reply ordering, per-character "talkativeness," auto-mode) on top of these cards. https://docs.sillytavern.app/
- **Character.AI practice (2026)** — persona craft wisdom worth adopting: optimal persona = **90–150 words, behavioral, first-person, key traits in the first sentence** (long biographies dilute signal); **pinned memories** (up to 15) keep pivotal facts permanently in context; 2026 brought a 3-layer memory system (persona / pinned / rolling). https://www.memorylake.ai/en/blogs/character-ai-forgets-persona · https://weavai.app/blog/en/2026/08/13/character-ais-new-3-layer-memory-system-explained/
- **TinyTroupe persona specs** (§3) are the most rigorous *programmatic* persona definition + adherence-correction machinery if we outgrow cards.
- No serious *new* cross-industry persona standard emerged in 2026 beyond CCv3; the action is in memory systems, not card schemas.

**Takeaway:** adopt CCv3/CHARX as the locked-host bundle format (it already handles group scenes, expressions, and conditional lore), write the core persona Character.AI-style (≤150 words, behavioral), and treat Neuro-sama's persona-in-weights fine-tuning as the eventual endgame.

## 6. Conversational TTS engines built for DIALOGUE realism

The architecture-changing category: **single-pass multi-speaker dialogue renderers** (whole conversation in one generation → the model itself times interruptions/overlaps/laughs). These, not per-line TTS, are how you get NotebookLM-grade texture.

### Commercial
- **ElevenLabs Eleven v3 + Text to Dialogue API** — public API now available; **audio tags** incl. turn-dynamics tags (`[interrupting]`, `[overlapping]`, `[cuts in]`), emotional tags (`[excited]`, `[annoyed]`), rhythm tags (`[hesitates]`, `[pause]`); **Dialogue mode takes a speaker-tagged script for multiple voices in one generation and renders real interruption timing** ("Maybe we should—" / "—Not yet" overlaps properly). Relevant since Robert's shop already runs ElevenLabs. https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/text-to-dialogue · https://elevenlabs.io/blog/eleven-v3-audio-tags-bringing-multi-character-dialogue-to-life · https://elevenlabs.io/blog/v3-audiotags · ~$0.10/1K chars API. Caveat: v3 is an expressive *render* model, not for realtime.
- **Gemini TTS (Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS, 2026)** — **native multi-speaker dialogue TTS via API** ("generate two-person NotebookLM-style audio overviews from text"), audio tags for vocal style/pace, "director's notes" style control, 70+ languages. NOTE: the aiobr environment already uses `google/gemini-3.1-flash-tts` (gemini-fast-tts skill) — meaning a NotebookLM-lineage multi-speaker dialogue renderer is ALREADY in the toolchain. https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/speech-generation · https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-1-flash-tts/ · https://blog.google/technology/google-deepmind/gemini-2-5-native-audio/
- **Hume Octave** — best-in-class emotion/prosody control; single-speaker orientation → good for a host voice, not the ensemble layer. https://futureagi.com/blog/best-voice-ai-may-2026/
- **Cartesia Sonic 4** — latency king (~40ms TTFA) for realtime agents; not a dialogue-ensemble renderer.
- **PlayHT/PlayAI "Dialog"** — was the podcast-dialogue specialist; **acquired by Meta July 2025 and being wound down. AVOID building on it.** https://futureagi.com/blog/best-voice-ai-may-2026/

### Open source (we run local — these matter most)
- **MOSS-TTSD v1.0 (OpenMOSS, Feb 2026)** — the current open-weights leader for spoken dialogue: **8B (+1.7B) Apache-2.0, 1–5 speakers, up to 60 min single pass, natural turn-taking AND overlapping speech, zero-shot voice cloning, 20 languages**; scripts as `[S1]…[S2]…` tagged text; claims parity with ElevenLabs/Gemini and wins vs VibeVoice/FireRedTTS-2 on speaker-attribution/WER benchmarks. https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOSS-TTSD · family: https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOSS-TTS · paper (Mar 2026): https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19739
- **Microsoft VibeVoice** (open-sourced 2025-08-25; ICLR 2026 Oral) — long-form multi-speaker TTS: **up to 90 minutes, up to 4 speakers, single pass**, expressive podcast-style audio, 7.5Hz continuous tokenizer. https://github.com/microsoft/VibeVoice · https://microsoft.github.io/VibeVoice/
- **SoulX-Podcast** (Soul AI Lab, Oct 2025) — long-form multi-speaker podcast synthesis with **paralinguistic tags and dialect diversity**, built explicitly for realistic podcasts. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23541
- **Higgs Audio V2** (Boson AI, open weights, Llama-derivative license) — 10M-hour-pretrained audio foundation model; **multi-speaker dialogue with natural pauses and overlaps**, strong emotion benchmarks (beats gpt-4o-mini-tts on Emotions 75.7%); Replicate-runnable. https://github.com/boson-ai/higgs-audio · https://www.boson.ai/blog/higgs-tts-2
- **Dia / Dia2 (Nari Labs)** — 1.6B Apache-2.0 dialogue model, one-pass multi-speaker with **nonverbal tags rendered as real audio events: (laughs), (coughs), (sighs)** — the thing ElevenLabs/CSM couldn't do at its release; Dia2 now on GitHub/HF via Transformers. https://github.com/nari-labs/dia · https://huggingface.co/nari-labs/Dia-1.6B
- **Sesame CSM** (Apache 2.0, CSM-1B open) — *context-conditioned* speech: feed prior utterances as context and delivery adapts (tone, pacing) to conversation flow; Llama backbone + Mimi codes. The open checkpoint is the small model; Sesame's magic demo ("Maya") remains closed. https://github.com/SesameAILabs/csm · https://learnprompting.org/blog/sesame-conversational-speech-model-open-sourced
- Also in the space: FireRedTTS-2 (podcast dialogue), MoonCast, CoVoMix (Microsoft research; parallel-channel simultaneous speakers — the academically cleanest overlap approach). https://arxiv.org/html/2510.23541v1

## TOP 5 — STEAL THIS (ranked by impact for our locked-host talk show)

**1. Move realism from the script to the audio layer: render whole scenes with a single-pass multi-speaker DIALOGUE model.** NotebookLM's disfluencies live in the audio model, not the transcript (Latent Space teardown) — so stop trying to write "um"/interruptions as text for per-line TTS. Feed a speaker-tagged scene to a dialogue renderer that times overlaps/laughs itself: **ElevenLabs v3 Text-to-Dialogue** (already in-house; `[interrupting]`/`[overlapping]`/`[laughs]` tags) for hero quality, **Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS** (already in the toolchain via Replicate) for cheap volume, **MOSS-TTSD / VibeVoice / Dia2** for local+free. This single change kills "everyone waits their turn."

**2. Steal the writers'-room + messiness-pass script pipeline.** PodAgent's Host–Guest–Writer decomposition (each host drafted *in persona* by its own agent, then a Writer compiles) beats one-shot generation measurably; then run a DUPLEXGEN-style post-pass: insert backchannels ("uh-huh," "right"), truncate a line and overlap the next speaker's barge-in, add hesitations. Two passes, both plain LLM calls — 90% of "sounds real" for the cost of a second prompt.

**3. Engineer disagreement and information-withholding as show mechanics (NotebookLM's own recipe).** "Too much agreement isn't fun" — give every episode a designed conflict axis between hosts (NotebookLM productized this as its Debate format), and script the reveal so hosts discover/withhold info across the episode instead of front-loading. Also adopt their taste rule: ban the tics reviewers flag as fake (catchphrases like "deep dive," constant validation, even turn lengths).

**4. Adopt Character Card V3 (CHARX) as the locked-host bundle format + Character.AI persona craft.** CCv3 already solves persona packaging for multi-character scenes: `group_only_greetings`, expression/audio assets in one zip, lorebook decorators for conditional running gags — and it's the community standard with tooling (SillyTavern group-chat logic = reference implementation for reply ordering/talkativeness). Write each core persona Character.AI-style: ≤150 words, behavioral, first-person, plus pinned "callback memories" per host that persist across episodes — persistent callbacks are what reviewers credit as "real" in Neuro-sama.

**5. Add a director/floor-manager agent for who-speaks-next and when-to-interrupt.** Round-robin turn allocation is the #1 written-dialogue tell. AG2's GroupChat selector pattern (a model picks the next speaker each beat), Concordia v2's Game-Master (sets scene, provokes, adjudicates), and "Learning to Interrupt" (arXiv 2604.06452) all point the same way: a lightweight director model that allocates turns unevenly, triggers barge-ins on hot lines, and cuts hosts off. Pairs with #1: the director emits the speaker-tagged, interruption-annotated scene that the dialogue renderer performs.

*(Market validation: Jellypod sells exactly this stack as a product — up to 4 persistent-persona hosts that "interrupt, react, and build on each other's points" — and Inception Point runs 120+ recurring personalities at 3,000 eps/week. The category is proven; the differentiator is host writing + the two-layer realism above.)*
